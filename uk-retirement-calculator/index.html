When not to rely on this estimate
This simple version is intended for supported standard pension-income examples. It does not model all tax reliefs, allowances, pension lump-sum effects, benefits, savings or investment income, foreign income, residence issues or cross-border tax rules. If you have other taxable income or a complex situation, use current HMRC guidance or speak to a qualified tax adviser.

How the estimate works

The calculator totals the pension income entered, estimates the Personal Allowance using the standard £12,570 allowance and the £1-for-£2 taper above £100,000 adjusted net income, then applies illustrative 2026–27 income tax bands for the selected jurisdiction. It assumes the entered pension income is the only taxable income included in the calculation. Scottish rates and bands differ from the rest of the UK.

Rules and thresholds can change. Confirm current rules with GOV.UK tax guidance before making decisions.

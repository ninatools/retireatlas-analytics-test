How to interpret your results

What does “today's dollars” mean?

Future balances are divided by the assumed inflation factor. This expresses the estimated balance as purchasing power in today's money, so it can be compared with today's spending target.

How is the savings projection calculated?

The calculator grows super and other savings using your assumed investment return. Employer contributions are estimated from salary and the entered super rate, with a 15% contributions-tax allowance. Extra annual savings are added separately. Contributions stop at your selected retirement age.

How is the Age Pension estimated?

The calculator estimates deemed income, then applies standard income and assets tests and selects the lower result. It uses official published rates and thresholds dated 20 September 2026 for standard domestic cases. It does not reproduce every Centrelink rule.

What should I do next?

Try lower and higher return and inflation assumptions, check how long your savings may need to last, and compare the results with your expected spending. Consider independent financial advice before making decisions.

Hua Hin

Hua Hin is a coastal town with a long-established domestic holiday scene, seafood markets, golf courses and residential areas that range from the busy centre to quieter outskirts. It offers beach access without the scale of Bangkok, although a car or taxi may be useful for errands outside central areas. Heat and humidity are persistent, and the sea is not equally suitable for swimming at all times of year. Check the location of hospitals, the quality of local transport and how easily you can reach Bangkok for complex specialist care.

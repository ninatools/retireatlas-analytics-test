About RetireAtlas

RetireAtlas is an independent educational project designed to help people explore retirement planning with clearer information and practical calculators.

Our tools currently include retirement savings estimates for the United States and Australia, a UK pension tax illustration, and a guide to retirement destinations across eight countries.

Our approach

  • Present assumptions and limitations clearly.
  • Link to official sources where practical, so readers can check rules and current requirements.
  • Help users compare scenarios without promising outcomes or replacing professional advice.
  • Keep tools accessible and usable on mobile devices.

Important note

RetireAtlas calculators and destination guides are for general educational purposes. They are not financial, tax, immigration, medical, or legal advice. Rules, costs, visa conditions, and benefits can change; verify important decisions with the relevant official authority or a qualified professional.

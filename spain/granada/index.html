Granada

Inland Andalusian city near the Sierra Nevada, known for historic quarters, gardens and university life.

Photo: Alhambra evening panorama Mirador San Nicolas sRGB-1 by Slaunger · CC BY-SA 3.0. Image resized/cropped for layout.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Inland Andalusian city near the Sierra Nevada, known for historic quarters, gardens and university life.
  • Municipality area: about 88 km² (approximate; municipality boundary, not the built-up urban area).
  • Population: about 235,000 residents (municipality, rounded).

Population refers to the municipality, not the wider metropolitan area. Spain's official municipal population data are published by the National Statistics Institute (INE). INE population data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Granada has a wider seasonal range than many coastal cities: summers can be very hot and winter nights cold. Some historic lanes are steep.

The city offers cultural life and mountain access, with many everyday services in a compact area. Check slopes, public transport, winter heating and the distance to healthcare from your chosen neighbourhood.

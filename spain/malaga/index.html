Málaga

Andalusian city on Spain’s southern Mediterranean coast, with a historic centre, port, beaches and airport and rail connections.

Photo: Málaga City by Triptosia · CC BY-SA 4.0. Image resized/cropped for layout.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Andalusian city on Spain’s southern Mediterranean coast, with a historic centre, port, beaches and airport and rail connections.
  • Municipality area: about 398 km² (approximate; municipality boundary, not the built-up urban area).
  • Population: about 592,000 residents (municipality, rounded).

Population refers to the municipality, not the wider metropolitan area. Spain's official municipal population data are published by the National Statistics Institute (INE). INE population data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Winters are generally mild; summer heat can be intense. Seasonal visitor demand affects some districts and services.

Málaga offers museums, cultural events and coastal access. Compare housing costs, tourist-season noise, walkability and the actual journey from your home to transport and healthcare.

Panama city guide

Panama City

Capital city and the country's broadest range of urban services

Research note: This page is for general retirement research, not personal financial, immigration or medical advice. Check current official information and visit the area before making decisions.
Photo: Casco Viejo, Panama City, Panama by rb-photo · CC BY 2.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout.

Local setting and everyday life

Panama City combines a modern skyline, historic Casco Antiguo, restaurants, shopping, international flights and the country's broadest concentration of specialist medical services. Neighbourhoods differ greatly: some are walkable and close to services, while others are car-oriented and separated by busy roads. Heat, humidity, traffic and heavy seasonal rain can affect everyday routines. Compare building quality, flood risk, noise and travel times to the specific hospital you expect to use.

Hot and humid tropical climate with a pronounced rainy season. Heavy rain, traffic and heat can influence daily travel.

Quick facts and population context

Population context: the capital district and metropolitan area have different totals. PROPANAMA cites about 1.55 million people in the metropolitan area; this is not the population of the historic central city. Area: depends on whether city, district or metro boundaries are used.

Panama uses the US dollar alongside the balboa at a fixed 1:1 parity. Costs, transport and access to services vary by neighbourhood and season.

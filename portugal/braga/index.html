Braga

Northern Portugal, with a historic centre, university areas and modern residential districts.

Photo: Braga Panorama by Otto Domes · CC BY-SA 4.0. Image resized/cropped for layout.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Northern Portugal, with a historic centre, university areas and modern residential districts.
  • Municipality area: about 183 km² (approximate; municipal boundary, not the built-up urban area).
  • Population: about 193,000 residents (2021 Census, rounded; municipality figure).

Population and area are approximate municipality-level context, not a measure of the wider urban or metropolitan population. Population reference: Portugal 2021 Census (INE); area reference: official administrative boundaries. Population data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Winters are generally wetter and cooler than in the Algarve; summers can be hot. Rainfall and hills affect walking and travel routines.

Braga combines everyday urban services, cafés, shopping and cultural life. Explore the exact neighbourhood on foot and check public transport and accessibility before choosing a home.

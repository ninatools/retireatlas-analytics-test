Funchal

Madeira's capital on the island's south coast, with a waterfront centre and steep hillside neighbourhoods.

Photo: View of Funchal. Portugal, Autonomous Region of Madeira, Southwestern Europe by Mstyslav Chernov · CC BY-SA 3.0. Image resized/cropped for layout.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Madeira's capital on the island's south coast, with a waterfront centre and steep hillside neighbourhoods.
  • Municipality area: about 76 km² (approximate; municipal boundary, not the built-up urban area).
  • Population: about 105,000 residents (2021 Census, rounded; municipality figure).

Population and area are approximate municipality-level context, not a measure of the wider urban or metropolitan population. Population reference: Portugal 2021 Census (INE); area reference: official administrative boundaries. Population data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Generally mild maritime conditions, with local temperatures and rainfall changing with elevation; steep streets and sudden weather changes matter.

Funchal has Madeira's broadest concentration of services, markets and cultural activities. Island living can mean travelling to mainland Portugal for some specialist treatment or family visits.

Lisbon

Portugal's capital on the Tagus estuary, with historic districts, waterfront areas and extensive metropolitan transport.

Photo: Yellow tram in Lisbon, Portugal by Bex Walton · CC BY 2.0. Image resized/cropped for layout.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Portugal's capital on the Tagus estuary, with historic districts, waterfront areas and extensive metropolitan transport.
  • Municipality area: about 100 km² (approximate; municipal boundary, not the built-up urban area).
  • Population: about 546,000 residents (2021 Census, rounded; municipality figure).

Population and area are approximate municipality-level context, not a measure of the wider urban or metropolitan population. Population reference: Portugal 2021 Census (INE); area reference: official administrative boundaries. Population data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Hills, older pavements, tourist crowds and summer heat can affect accessibility.

Lisbon offers extensive cultural life, international connections and specialist services. Housing can be expensive; compare lift access, neighbourhood noise, daily shops and the journey to healthcare.

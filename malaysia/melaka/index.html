Melaka

Historic coastal city and state capital on the Strait of Malacca.

Photo: Melaka-Dutch-Square-2164 by Vmenkov · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Historic coastal city and state capital on the Strait of Malacca.
  • Area: Melaka Tengah district covers the city and surrounding areas.
  • Population: Melaka Tengah had about 950,000 residents in 2020 (district-wide, not just the historic centre).

Malaysia’s official census publishes figures by administrative district and local authority; these are not always the same as a city’s built-up area. Avoid comparing figures across different boundary types. Department of Statistics Malaysia — Census 2020 ↗

Climate and everyday life

Hot, humid tropical weather throughout the year with frequent rain. The heritage core can be busy during weekends and holidays.

Compact historic streets, museums, riverside walks and a blend of cultural traditions. Compare quieter residential districts with the tourist centre and check nearby healthcare and transport.

Destination overview: Melaka offers a compact historic centre, riverside walks, museums and a strong mix of Malay, Chinese, Indian and Portuguese-influenced heritage. It is smaller and generally calmer than Kuala Lumpur, with a strong visitor economy and access to everyday services. Weekend and holiday traffic can make the centre crowded, while the hottest part of the day can be uncomfortable for long walks. Retirees should compare quieter residential districts with the tourist core, confirm the range of nearby healthcare services and check transport links for trips to larger cities.

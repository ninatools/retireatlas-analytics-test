Kuala Lumpur

Malaysia’s federal capital and a dense metropolitan centre.

Photo: Petronas twin towers by Honzasoukup · CC BY 3.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Malaysia’s federal capital and a dense metropolitan centre.
  • Area: about 243 km² (Federal Territory).
  • Population: about 1.98 million (2020 Census; Kuala Lumpur City Hall area).

Malaysia’s official census publishes figures by administrative district and local authority; these are not always the same as a city’s built-up area. Avoid comparing figures across different boundary types. Department of Statistics Malaysia — Census 2020 ↗

Climate and everyday life

Hot and humid throughout the year, with frequent heavy downpours. Shade, covered routes and rail access can make everyday travel easier.

Major private hospitals, rail transit, international flights and broad shopping and dining choices. Compare neighbourhoods for station access, noise, building accessibility and insurance acceptance.

Destination overview: Malaysia’s capital is the strongest choice for people who want a large city, major private hospitals, international flights, rail transit, shopping and a wide variety of restaurants. Neighbourhoods range from high-rise districts with extensive amenities to older areas with local markets and street life. The tropical heat and frequent downpours can make walking less comfortable, and many daily routines still depend on cars or ride-hailing outside rail corridors. Check the actual walking route to stations, building accessibility, traffic noise and the insurance arrangements required by hospitals.

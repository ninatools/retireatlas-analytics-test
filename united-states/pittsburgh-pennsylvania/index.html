Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Western Pennsylvania city at the confluence of three rivers.

Photo: Pittsburgh skyline view by Derek Cashman · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Western Pennsylvania city at the confluence of three rivers.
  • Area: about 151 km² (city limits).
  • Population: about 303,000 (2020 Census; city proper).

Population and area refer to city boundaries, not the wider metropolitan area. Population year varies where the latest official estimate is cited. U.S. Census Bureau data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Four distinct seasons, with cold winters, occasional snow and warm, humid summers. Hills and winter conditions can affect walking and driving.

A regional centre with universities, cultural venues and major medical institutions. Compare neighbourhoods for steep streets, transit, winter accessibility and housing costs.

Destination overview: A regional city with major medical and university institutions, established neighbourhoods and riverfront scenery. Research winter weather, steep streets, transport links and neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood housing costs.

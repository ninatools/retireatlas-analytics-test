Tucson, Arizona

Southern Arizona city in the Sonoran Desert, surrounded by mountain ranges.

Photo: A view of a city with mountains in the background by JC Cervantes · Unsplash License · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Southern Arizona city in the Sonoran Desert, surrounded by mountain ranges.
  • Area: about 620 km² (city limits).
  • Population: about 548,000 (2025 estimate; city proper).

Population and area refer to city boundaries, not the wider metropolitan area. Population year varies where the latest official estimate is cited. U.S. Census Bureau data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Desert climate with very hot summers, a sunny dry season and a summer monsoon period. Heat safety, shade, air conditioning and water conditions deserve attention.

Mountain scenery, university and regional medical services. Compare neighbourhoods for access to care and transport, and plan for extreme summer heat and cooling costs.

Destination overview: A desert city with mountain scenery and a sunny, dry climate for much of the year. Research extreme summer temperatures, air-conditioning costs, water conditions, transport and access to the medical specialists you may need.

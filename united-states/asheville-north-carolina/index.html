Asheville, North Carolina

Smaller mountain city in western North Carolina near the Blue Ridge Mountains.

Photo: Downtown Asheville by mdl70 · CC BY-SA 2.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Smaller mountain city in western North Carolina near the Blue Ridge Mountains.
  • Area: about 117 km² (city limits).
  • Population: about 94,000 (2020 Census; city proper).

Population and area refer to city boundaries, not the wider metropolitan area. Population year varies where the latest official estimate is cited. U.S. Census Bureau data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Mountain-influenced four-season climate, generally cooler than lowland parts of the Southeast, with summer humidity and potential severe-weather impacts.

Outdoor recreation and a compact urban core appeal to some retirees. Compare housing affordability, access to specialists, road conditions and local flood or storm exposure.

Destination overview: A smaller city near the Blue Ridge Mountains that may appeal to people who value outdoor recreation and a milder mountain setting. Check housing affordability, specialist healthcare access, road conditions and exposure to severe weather or flooding.

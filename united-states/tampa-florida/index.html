Tampa, Florida

Gulf Coast metropolitan city in west-central Florida.

Photo: Tampa Skyline by Sonny SideUp · CC BY 2.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Gulf Coast metropolitan city in west-central Florida.
  • Area: about 455 km² (city limits, including water area).
  • Population: about 385,000 (2020 Census; city proper).

Population and area refer to city boundaries, not the wider metropolitan area. Population year varies where the latest official estimate is cited. U.S. Census Bureau data ↗

Climate and everyday life

Hot, humid summers and mild winters; hurricane season, heavy rain and flooding are important planning considerations.

Waterfront areas, urban amenities and regional healthcare. Check flood risk by property, home insurance costs, hurricane preparedness, transport and health-insurance affordability.

Destination overview: A warm-weather metropolitan area with waterfront districts, urban amenities and access to regional healthcare. Compare home and flood insurance, hurricane exposure, summer heat, transport and the cost of medical coverage.

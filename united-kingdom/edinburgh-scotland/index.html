Edinburgh, Scotland

Scotland’s capital, with historic districts and a major cultural and education sector.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Scotland’s capital, with historic districts and a major cultural and education sector.
  • Area: about 264 km² (City of Edinburgh council area).
  • Population: about 518,000 (2022 Census; council area).

Climate and everyday life

Cool temperate climate with changeable weather, cool winters and mild summers. Wind, rain, hills and steps can affect everyday mobility.

Museums, rail links, universities and a broad range of city services. Compare housing costs, accessibility of older streets and travel time to the hospital you prefer.

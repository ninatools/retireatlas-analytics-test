York, England

Historic city in North Yorkshire, northern England.

Quick facts

  • Setting: Historic city in North Yorkshire, northern England.
  • Area: about 272 km² (City of York local authority).
  • Population: about 202,800 (2021 Census; local authority).

Population figures use the stated administrative or built-up boundary and are rounded; these boundaries differ across the UK. Official census data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Cool temperate conditions with relatively mild summers and cool, damp winters. Heavy rain can affect riverside and low-lying areas.

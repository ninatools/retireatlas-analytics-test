Bath, England

Historic city in Somerset, South West England.

Photo: Royal Crescent, Bath by MontanNito · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Historic city in Somerset, South West England.
  • Area: about 29 km² for the built-up city; wider Bath and North East Somerset authority is much larger.
  • Population: about 94,000 (2021 Census; built-up area, rounded).

Population figures use the stated administrative or built-up boundary and are rounded; these boundaries differ across the UK. Official census data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Generally mild, with wet weather possible throughout the year. The hilly terrain and sloping streets can matter for walking and mobility.

Georgian architecture, cultural venues and green spaces. Housing can be expensive relative to nearby areas; consider hills, steps, tourist activity and access to specialist care.

Destination overview: A compact historic city known for Georgian architecture, cultural life and green spaces. Compare housing costs, hills and steps in older neighbourhoods, tourist-season activity and travel to specialist services.

Mérida

Yucatán Peninsula; capital city and regional service centre.

Photo: Paseo de Montejo, Merida Yucatan Mexico 01 by Nils & Araceli Jonsson · CC BY-SA 2.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Yucatán Peninsula; capital city and regional service centre.
  • Area: about 858 km² (administrative municipality/entity, not just the built-up centre).
  • Population: about 995,000 (2020 Census; municipality).

Population figures are rounded and refer to the administrative area stated, not necessarily the urban core. Reference: INEGI Census 2020 / official geographic data. Official data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Hot, humid conditions for much of the year, with especially intense heat before the rainy season. Air conditioning, shade and heat planning can matter for daily comfort.

A historic centre, markets, cultural events and a broad range of urban services. Compare neighbourhoods for walkability, traffic, heat exposure and distance to specialist care.

Destination overview: Mérida, the capital of Yucatán, is known for its historic centre, markets, cultural events and strong regional identity. It has grown into a service centre for the surrounding peninsula, with restaurants, shops and private medical options. The major consideration is climate: heat and humidity can be intense, particularly in the hottest months, so air conditioning, shade and reliable transport may be essential. Research neighbourhoods outside the busiest centre as well as access to specialist care and the distance to the coast.

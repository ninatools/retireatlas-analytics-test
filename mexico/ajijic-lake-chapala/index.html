Ajijic and Lake Chapala

Lakeside community on Lake Chapala, in Chapala municipality, Jalisco.

Photo: Lago de Chapala desde el muelle de Ajijic by YdelaT · CC0 1.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Lakeside community on Lake Chapala, in Chapala municipality, Jalisco.
  • Area: Chapala municipality about 385 km² (administrative municipality/entity, not just the built-up centre).
  • Population: about 55,000 (2020 Census; Chapala municipality, not Ajijic alone).

Population figures are rounded and refer to the administrative area stated, not necessarily the urban core. Reference: INEGI Census 2020 / official geographic data. Official data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Highland lakeside weather is generally more moderate than the coast, with rainy-season showers and cooler evenings.

A small-town setting, lakeside scenery and community activities. Some complex medical needs may require travel to Guadalajara. Check transport, air quality, water supply and the location of the nearest suitable hospital.

Destination overview: Ajijic and neighbouring communities along Lake Chapala attract people who want a small-town setting, lakeside scenery and social activities rather than a large metropolis. The highland location generally offers more moderate temperatures than the coast, though rainy-season weather and cooler nights still matter. Local services are available, but complex medical needs may require travel to Guadalajara. Before settling, check transport options, air quality, water supply, the practicalities of the nearest hospital and whether the community feels comfortable outside the main expat areas.

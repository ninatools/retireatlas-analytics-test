Puerto Vallarta

Pacific coast on Banderas Bay, Jalisco.

Photo: El Malecón Puerto Vallarta (228717327) by Terry Lucas · CC BY 3.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Pacific coast on Banderas Bay, Jalisco.
  • Area: about 1,101 km² (administrative municipality/entity, not just the built-up centre).
  • Population: about 292,000 (2020 Census; municipality).

Population figures are rounded and refer to the administrative area stated, not necessarily the urban core. Reference: INEGI Census 2020 / official geographic data. Official data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Tropical coastal weather with hot, humid summers and a rainy season; beach conditions and local travel can vary seasonally.

A seafront promenade, restaurants, outdoor activities and international air links. Busy districts may cost more. Check flood exposure, transport, and distance from home to hospitals and daily shops.

Destination overview: Puerto Vallarta combines a Pacific bay, a walkable seafront promenade, restaurants, arts and access to outdoor activities. It has an established international community and air links that can be convenient for visitors and family. The climate is tropical, with a hotter, wetter period and seasonal changes in beach conditions; popular districts can be busy and more expensive than inland areas. Check the distance from a potential home to hospitals, grocery shops and transport, and consider how you would manage during the rainy season.

Mexico City

Large high-altitude capital and metropolitan centre.

Photo: Palacio de Bellas Artes, Mexico City, MX by Timothy Neesam · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Quick facts

  • Setting: Large high-altitude capital and metropolitan centre.
  • Area: about 1,495 km² (administrative municipality/entity, not just the built-up centre).
  • Population: about 9.2 million (2020 Census; Mexico City federal entity).

Population figures are rounded and refer to the administrative area stated, not necessarily the urban core. Reference: INEGI Census 2020 / official geographic data. Official data source ↗

Climate and everyday life

Mild-to-warm days and cooler evenings due to elevation, with a distinct rainy season. Air quality and altitude may affect some residents.

A wide choice of museums, parks, food, public transport and medical services. Neighbourhoods differ greatly; assess noise, walkability, air quality and realistic journey times.

Destination overview: Mexico City offers exceptional choice in museums, food, parks, hospitals, public transport and international connections. Its size means neighbourhoods can feel like distinct small cities, so walkability, noise, air quality, elevation and travel times vary widely. It may suit retirees who want a rich urban routine and a broad range of specialist services, but traffic and the scale of the city can be tiring. Visit the specific districts you would consider living in and test ordinary journeys before deciding.

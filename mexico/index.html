Mexico

A large and diverse country of colonial cities, highland towns and Pacific and Caribbean coastlines.

Photo: Mexico City Reforma skyline by Jonathan Salvador · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Image resized/cropped for layout

Mexico is not one retirement experience: altitude, climate, language, transport and access to medical care can be very different from one region to another. Highland cities may have mild days and cooler nights, while coastal destinations are often hot and humid. Larger cities and established retirement communities may offer private hospitals, international connections and social networks, but the right choice depends on the neighbourhood and the services you personally need. Learn enough Spanish for everyday tasks, check current official travel and safety advice for the specific state and city, and investigate residency, health insurance and tax matters independently. Renting for a season before buying can help you experience the climate and daily routine beyond the holiday areas.
